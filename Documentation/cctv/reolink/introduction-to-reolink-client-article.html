Introduction to Reolink Client (New Client)

Reolink Client helps you keep an eye on your home and business whenever and wherever you are.

Note: you can download it on the Download Center.

The operation page on Reolink Client can be divided into three parts mainly, the Live View page, the Playback page, and the Device Settings page.

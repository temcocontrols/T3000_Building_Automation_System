# Exporting Clips & Cloud Archiving with UniFi Protect

With UniFi Protect, you have two powerful ways to manage your recordings: export clips for immediate sharing and archive footage for secure, long-term storage. Together, these options ensure your recordings are accessible whenever you need them. 
## Exporting Clips 

Exporting lets you download individual video clips for quick sharing, such as providing evidence or sharing specific events. 
- Mobile App: Tap the Share icon and select Export clip . 
- Web Browser: In the Playback or Find Anything tab, select the clip and click the Download icon. 
## Cloud Archiving 

Archiving enables long-term storage of recordings without using up space on your NVR. Footage can be archived to the following destinations: 
- Google Drive 
- OneDrive 
- Dropbox 
- NAS 
Setup: Activate cloud archiving via Settings > Off-Site Archiving in the Protect web app.
